= Applied Network Programmability: Automating Cisco Infrastructure with Python, APIs, and NetDevOps

*Author:* Dr. Frazier A. Smith, Central Piedmont Community College

== About This Book

_Applied Network Programmability: Automating Cisco Infrastructure with Python, APIs, and NetDevOps_ is an Open Educational Resource (OER) textbook designed for students pursuing practical, enterprise-grade competency in network automation, infrastructure as code, and modern software-defined networking. The book is aligned to the Cisco Certified DevNet Associate (200-901 DEVASC) certification and Quality Matters 7th Edition Higher Education standards, covering the full breadth of modern network programmability—from foundational Linux environments and Python data structures to RESTful APIs, containerized microservices, Ansible orchestration, and model-driven telemetry using YANG, NETCONF, and RESTCONF.

This textbook takes a hands-on, applied approach. Rather than treating network programmability in the abstract, every chapter grounds its content in authentic enterprise scenarios, connects directly to the NETLAB+ DEVASC Workstation environment and Cisco DevNet Sandboxes, and embeds bespoke instructional video explainers, screencasts, and packet walkthroughs. Students apply their learning incrementally across an 8-week authentic individual capstone: the *Network Change Automation Project*.

The book follows an 8-chapter sequence spanning eight weeks of instruction:
